### 1. AI & Stable Diffusion
* **Error:** `LayerNormKernelImpl not implemented for Half`
* **Solution:** Add `--precision full --no-half` to your launch arguments. [Full Guide Here](https://kalida.top/fix/stable-diffusion-layernormkernelimpl-not-implemented-fix)

### 2. WSL 2 & Virtualization
* **Error:** `0x80040326 (CreateVM Failed)`

* **Solution:** Use `--cookies-from-browser` flag to bypass YouTube's bot detection. [Full Tutorial](https://kalida.top/fix/yt-dlp-sign-in-to-confirm-you-are-not-a-bot-fix)
